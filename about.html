Our Story

Built by people who hate parking too

ParkEase started from a single frustration at Phoenix MarketCity in 2023. Now we serve 50,000+ Chennaikars monthly.

The problem was obvious.
The solution wasn't.

In 2023, our founders Arjun and Meera spent 28 minutes circling 5 floors of Phoenix MarketCity only to discover they were full. They left without shopping.

Existing solutions — LCD boards at entrances, WhatsApp groups for "tips" — were all manual, slow, and unreliable. The problem wasn't just inconvenience. It was lost revenue for malls, wasted fuel, and frustrated customers.

We built ParkEase in 90 days using IoT sensors, a simple REST API, and a web app that any smartphone could access without an install. The first pilot was at Express Avenue in January 2024.

Today, we're live at 6 malls with 2,400+ monitored slots, and we're expanding to Bengaluru and Hyderabad in Q2 2025.

"Nobody should drive to a mall and leave without shopping because they couldn't find parking. That's revenue Chennai is losing every day."

— Arjun Mehta, Co-founder
Our Mission

To make every parking experience in Indian cities predictable, fair, and frictionless — starting with Chennai's malls and expanding citywide.
